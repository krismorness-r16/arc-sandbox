Where the prompts sit — try it

Four values place every prompt. Drag them and watch the arc rebuild; the maths is the same as the shipping layout, including the end-to-end spread and which side the text runs to.

    Zero degrees is right and angles increase anticlockwise, so the 90° default puts the arc overhead. A prompt past a quarter turn is on the left half, where its text runs the other way so the words point away from the thing they belong to — drag the centre past 90° and watch the labels flip. Buttons drawn with a ring are the holdable ones, and a dashed circle is a spacer — it takes a place on the arc and draws nothing in game, which is how the real prompts get pushed apart. Reorder the list to see where a gap lands.
